# Data

## Datasets Used

This project uses public procurement data from the City and County of San Francisco.

### 1. Procurement Contracts Data

Contains information about procurement contracts issued by the City and County of San Francisco.

The dataset is used to examine contract-level information and connect procurement activity with departments, suppliers, and spending.

### 2. Procurement Payments Data

Contains payment-level records associated with San Francisco procurement activity.

The dataset is used to analyze actual payments and spending patterns across suppliers, departments, procurement objects, and time periods.

## Data Usage

The datasets were used for:

* Data cleaning and preparation in Python
* Procurement spend analysis
* Supplier and department analysis
* Procurement category analysis
* Power BI dashboard development

The original datasets are publicly available through the City and County of San Francisco's open-data platform.

## Data Privacy

The project uses publicly available government procurement data. No private or personally identifiable information was added to the analysis.
